Controls

Gamepads: left stick flies, right stick X is rudder, triggers move the throttle, bumpers flaps, A gear, B brakes, X autopilot, Y camera. Joysticks with a throttle lever are detected automatically.

Quick start: your first takeoff

  1. Release the parking brake (Shift+B) and press Shift+T (TO/GA) — the autothrottle sets takeoff thrust.
  2. At VR (green bug on the speed tape, "rotate" callout) pull back (↓) smoothly to ~15° nose up, following the magenta flight director.
  3. After "positive rate" raise the gear (G). Above 1000 ft retract flaps on schedule (UP/1/5 bugs on the speed tape).
  4. Engage the autopilot (A), then LNAV (N) and LVL CHG (L) to climb to the MCP altitude along the route.
  5. For landing, press APP (M) when on a heading to intercept the localizer, extend flaps 30, gear down, arm speedbrake (K), autobrake 2 — the autopilot can autoland.
