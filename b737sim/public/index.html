Controls

Gamepads: left stick flies, right stick X is rudder, triggers move the throttle, bumpers flaps, A gear, B brakes, X autopilot, Y camera. Joysticks with a throttle lever are detected automatically. On touch screens use the stick at the right, the levers at the sides and the buttons along the bottom.

Quick start: departing KDFW

  1. Release the parking brake (Shift+B) and press Shift+T (TO/GA): the autothrottle sets takeoff thrust.
  2. At VR (green bug, "rotate" callout) pull back (↓) smoothly to about 15° nose up, following the magenta flight director.
  3. After "positive rate" raise the gear (G). Above 1000 ft retract the flaps on schedule (UP/1/5 bugs on the speed tape).
  4. Engage the autopilot (A), then LNAV (N) and VNAV (Shift+N) to fly the real SID and route.
  5. On the STAR, set the MCP altitude down for VNAV. Arm APP (M) when cleared: the FMC has tuned the real ILS. Flaps 30, gear down, speedbrake armed (K), autobrake 2; the autopilot can autoland.
  6. Open RADIOS to tune real VORs (try the Ranger VOR, FUZ 115.7) and WX for the METAR and ATIS. The ND shows weather radar returns (W).
